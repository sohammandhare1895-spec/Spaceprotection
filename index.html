ASTRONAUT HEALTH INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM

Long-duration missions expose astronauts to radiation, isolation, altered gravity and closed environments. ASTRA monitors health indicators locally on-device and provides decision-support when Earth communication is delayed.

◉ Live Camera ◉ HUD Telemetry ◉ AI Health Copilot ◉ Emergency Mode

Camera access is requested only after you click the button. All processing is local.

◉

CAMERA PERMISSION REQUIRED

ASTRA uses your device's rear camera to render the first-person astronaut visor view. Your camera feed is processed only on this device and is never uploaded.

Troubleshooting:

  • Ensure the page is served over https:// or localhost.
  • iOS Safari: Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow
  • Chrome: click the lock icon → Site settings → Camera → Allow
